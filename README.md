# mcChill
IoT Low-Cost Wireless Remote Temperature Monitoring Device for Vaccine Storage and Handling 

(Just a quick, executive project summary)

Our product, the mcChill, is an ultra low-power IoT wireless temperature monitoring device for vaccine storage and handling. Developed to address AHS and ACPs strict regulations[1], mcChill addresses limitations of devices currently on the market - such as high cost, large form factor, non-replaceable batteries, and a lack of real-time temperature alerts - we offer an affordable and scalable alternative. 

Our solution splits the problem into two parts, a BLE enabled temperature sensor paired with an LTE gateway which periodically sends temperature data to the cloud for automatic real-time logging and alerting. Through careful utilization of a NIST traceable temperature sensor - requiring no calibration or configuration - and onboard data storage, we accurately record and retain information until successful cloud storage. This ensures continuous and reliable temperature monitoring without data loss. By using a mains-connected gateway to support LTE’s high energy demands, data can be sent to the gateway over low-power BLE, maximizing the battery life of the sensor. Custom firmware and BLE antenna support battery life over a year in the 2-8oC environment of a vaccine storage fridge. Even our carefully designed mechanical features such as the ultrasonic weld, gasket+epoxy sealing, and unique multi-board-array PCB design keeps the device reliable, manufacturable, and scalable.

The outstanding performance of mcChill has been validated through stringent testing, achieving >99% uptime. Our cloud-based real-time temperature monitoring and alerting system through mcCloud reduces the risk of vaccine spoilage for Pharmacists while minimizing their operational costs. Designed from the ground up with manufacturability at its core, the product can be efficiently manufactured at scale for ~$11.03 per unit.
